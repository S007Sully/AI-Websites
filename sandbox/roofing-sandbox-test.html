FAQ

Questions homeowners ask.

Don’t see yours? Call (555) 010-0199.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

It depends on the age, condition and extent of the damage. An assessment shows whether a targeted repair makes sense or whether replacement is the better long-term value, and you get the reasoning in writing.

What happens during a roof assessment?

We inspect the roof surface, flashing, vents and visible decking, take photos, and walk you through the findings. You then receive a written estimate for any recommended work.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Timelines depend on roof size, materials and weather. Your estimate includes the expected schedule so you can plan around it.

Can you help with storm or hail damage and insurance?

We can inspect and document damage with photos to support your claim. Coverage decisions are made by your insurance company.

What warranties come with the work?

Warranty terms depend on the materials selected and the workmanship covered. Details are provided in your written estimate before you decide.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Your City and surrounding areas. Not sure if you're in range? Call or send a request and we'll confirm.
